# Demo College Attendance Policy — Hackathon Prototype

> [!NOTE]
> This document contains simulated prototype policies created solely for the AI Attendance Recovery Agent hackathon demonstration. It does not represent official university or college regulations.

## 1. Examination Eligibility Criteria
- A student is deemed eligible to appear for semester examinations if their **overall cumulative attendance is at least 80.0%** across all subjects combined.
- The 80.0% requirement is evaluated across the entire set of conducted classes up to the examination cutoff date.

## 2. Subject-Wise Attendance vs. Overall Eligibility
- Subject-wise attendance percentages are tracked and displayed to give students detailed visibility into their individual course participation.
- However, final examination eligibility is determined strictly on the **overall aggregate attendance** across all 8 equal subjects.
- A deficit in a specific subject can impact overall percentage, making consistent attendance across all subjects essential.

## 3. Attendance Threshold Monitoring
- Students with overall attendance **below 80.0%** are flagged for attendance shortage and recovery planning.
- Students maintaining overall attendance at or above **80.0%** meet the prototype eligibility benchmark.
